Questions about IT support in Falkirk

Do you help home users as well as businesses?

Yes. You can ask about a personal Windows computer, Microsoft 365, email or a small-business IT setup. Describe what you need so I can confirm whether it is something I can help with.

Can you visit my home or business?

In-person help is available by arrangement within 10 miles of Falkirk, Scotland. Please include your town or postcode when asking about a visit.

How much does support cost?

Initial advice is free. The scope and any costs for further work are agreed before starting. Equipment, software subscriptions, domains and hosting may involve separate third-party costs.

Do you offer emergency or guaranteed same-day support?

I do not promise emergency or same-day availability. Email your requirements and we can discuss a suitable time and approach.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Explain the problem, the device or software involved, what you have already tried, and your location. Mention whether you prefer remote help or a visit. Do not send passwords, access codes or confidential files.
