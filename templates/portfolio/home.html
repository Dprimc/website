Sometimes—and it can be a great learning project. But a home server also needs reliable power and internet, backups, updates and secure configuration.

We'll compare home hosting with a managed host for your situation. The lowest upfront cost isn't always the simplest or most dependable choice for a public business website.

Good questions to ask first

Before you buy anything

How much does it cost to get started?

It depends on your country and the tools you choose. A domain is commonly renewed yearly; email, hosting and AI tools can add recurring charges. We'll separate one-off and ongoing costs so you can decide what you actually need.

Do I need to buy a new computer or server?

Usually, start with the computer you already have. A home server can be useful for learning, but public hosting also needs reliable power and internet, backups, updates and security. We'll compare that with managed hosting before you decide.

Do I need to know how to build a website?

No. The guidance is designed for beginners and explains each step in plain English. The goal is for you to understand the setup, not just copy a list of instructions.

Is the intro call free?

Yes. The first conversation is free and there's no obligation. We'll clarify your goal and the next sensible step. Any further work and any costs are agreed in advance.

Can you help if I'm outside Scotland?

Remote guidance is available worldwide by arrangement. We'll confirm that the task, time zone and available tools are a good fit before going further.
